QUICK START
-----------
Mac: double click start.command (you may need to allow it in Privacy and Security),
or open Terminal here and run: python3 serve.py

Windows: double click start.bat,
or open PowerShell here and run: py serve.py

Open the Local address the terminal prints, for example http://127.0.0.1:8080

The camera only works on a localhost or https address.
The trackers are fetched from the web the first time they are used, so stay connected.

FIRST FIVE MINUTES
------------------
1. It opens with your pointer playing a hand: move over the sensor view, press to pinch, and watch the meters and the poster
2. Click Camera, allow it, and try the same with your own hand
3. On the right, click another suggestion, for example Face type, and use your face
4. Press the plus beside any signal to make your own rule, then choose what it drives
5. Press Space to Perform, and R to record a video of it
6. Bottom right: choose Camera and poster to save both scenes side by side
